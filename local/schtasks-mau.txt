LENH MAU DANG KY 2 TASK LAPTOP (CHUA dang ky - chay khi da seed Drive va cloud chay on dinh)
Mo PowerShell/CMD (khong can admin), dan tung dong:

schtasks /Create /F /TN "Pipeline Local Cvhcm" /SC DAILY /ST 08:30 /TR "powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -WindowStyle Hidden -File D:\cloud-deploy\local\Run-Local-Cvhcm.ps1"

schtasks /Create /F /TN "Pipeline Local Mini"  /SC DAILY /ST 18:40 /TR "powershell.exe -NoProfile -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -WindowStyle Hidden -File D:\cloud-deploy\local\Run-Local-Mini.ps1"

Kiem tra / chay thu / xoa:
  schtasks /Query /TN "Pipeline Local Mini" /V /FO LIST
  schtasks /Run   /TN "Pipeline Local Mini"
  schtasks /Delete /TN "Pipeline Local Mini" /F

Luu y:
- Task "Pipeline Local Mini" doi cloud Market PM xong (toi da 150 phut, tuc den ~21:10) roi moi chay; may phai bat
  trong khoang 18:40-21:10. Muon khong doi: them -NoWait vao cuoi /TR.
- Neu may thuong ngu: Task Scheduler -> Properties -> Conditions -> "Wake the computer to run this task";
  Settings -> "Run task as soon as possible after a scheduled start is missed".
- 4 task cu (Market Data AM/PM, Shipping AM/PM) TAT (Disable) chi khi cloud da chay on dinh >= 1 tuan:
  schtasks /Change /TN "Market Data AM" /DISABLE   (ten task xem trong Task Scheduler)
  Khong xoa, de bat lai duoc (schtasks /Change /TN "..." /ENABLE).
- Log: D:\pipeline-data\logs\local-mini_*.log, local-cvhcm_*.log; status: D:\pipeline-data\market-data\status-PM.txt,
  D:\pipeline-data\shipping\status-AM.txt
